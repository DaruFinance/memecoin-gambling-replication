# Study 3 registration: is retail speculation worse than gambling everywhere, and where does skill exist?

Status: frozen before any Hyperliquid or Polymarket outcome is computed (sha256 in `FREEZE.txt`).

**Disclosure.** Part of the Polymarket fill record was analysed earlier in a separate study (mid-2026). This study's Polymarket arm is therefore documented exposure, not a blind registration. No Hyperliquid wallet outcome has been seen.

## Question

1. Do retail traders in perpetual futures (Hyperliquid) and prediction markets (Polymarket) also get back less per unit at risk, and end ahead less often, than stake-matched gamblers? This is the same test as Study 2 for pump.fun.
2. In which venues does **skill** exist, meaning returns that are more dispersed than luck allows and that persist out of sample? Casinos have none by construction.

**Hyperliquid scope.** The primary is core perps: coins without a `:` deployer prefix. HIP-3 builder-deployed markets (`xyz:` etc.) and spot (`dir` Buy/Sell, outcome split/merge) are secondary or excluded.

## Bet units (what "staked" means in each venue)

- **Memecoins:** a Study 2 position. Stake = SOL paid including fees; X = cash out / cash in.
- **Perps: episode.** For each (wallet, coin), an episode runs from flat to flat, reconstructed from `startPosition` and signed size.
  - **Stake = peak |notional|** reached during the episode, the unlevered capital the same exposure would need. This makes a perp episode comparable to a spot bet.
  - **PnL** = Σ `closedPnl` − Σ (`fee` + `builderFee`) − funding paid. Funding = Σ over hours of position notional × that hour's funding rate (sign as paid).
  - **X = 1 + PnL / stake.**
  - An episode open at the window end is marked to the last fill price of that coin.
  - **Levered view (secondary):** an episode containing a `liquidation` fill is a total loss of its margin, reported as a liquidation-rate statistic.
- **Polymarket: position** = (wallet, outcome token).
  - Cash in = USDC spent buying.
  - Cash out = USDC from sells + redemption value of tokens still held (1 or 0 by resolution).
  - A position unresolved at the window end is marked to the last trade price.
  - X = out / in.
- **Per wallet (all venues):** ROI_w = Σ cash back / Σ stake. "Ahead" means ROI_w > 1.

## Who counts as retail

- **Memecoins:** as in Study 2.
- **Hyperliquid:** excluded are
  - HLP and vault addresses (from the public vault list);
  - wallets with more than 200 fills in any clock hour;
  - wallets with ≥ 80% maker (non-crossed) fills and ≥ 1,000 fills (market makers).
- **Polymarket:** excluded are wallets with more than 200 fills in any clock hour, or ≥ 80% maker-role fills and ≥ 1,000 fills.

The excluded classes are reported separately, with their net flows.

## Hypotheses

- **H1 (per venue):** pooled retail RTP < each casino game's RTP. Launch-day (memecoins) or calendar-day (perps, Polymarket) cluster bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, Holm across games.
- **H2 (per venue):** share of retail wallets ahead < share of stake-matched gamblers ahead. Same replay as Study 2, 200 draws per wallet; wallet bootstrap; Holm.
- **H4 (skill: excess dispersion, per venue):**
  - **No-skill null:** every retail position's X is replaced by a random draw (without replacement) from the venue's pooled retail X within the same stake decile and calendar day. Wallet ROI is then recomputed. 200 permutations.
  - **Statistic:** share of retail wallets with ≥ 10 positions whose observed ROI_w lies above the 95th percentile of their own null distribution.
  - Skill is present if that share's 95% wallet-bootstrap interval lies above 5%.
- **H5 (skill: persistence, per venue):**
  - Split each window into two equal halves.
  - Among retail wallets with ≥ 5 positions in each half, take the Spearman correlation of ROI_w in half 1 against half 2.
  - Also report the half-2 pooled RTP of half 1's top decile versus everyone else.
  - Null: the same statistics on the H4 permutation null.
  - Persistence is present if the observed correlation exceeds the 97.5th percentile of the null.

## How results are read

- A venue "is worse than gambling" on the same tiers as Study 2 (European roulette, Sweet Bonanza, every game), separately for H1 and H2.
- A venue "contains skill" if H4 or H5 holds.
- The expected and allowed pattern "negative-sum for retail on average **and** skilled minority present" is reported as such. It does not contradict H1/H2.
- Any venue whose retail RTP is at or above a game's RTP is reported as beating that game.

## Amendment S3-A1 (2026-10-10, before any Study 3 outcome computed in this project)

**Polymarket uses an existing position table** (one row per wallet × market × outcome over 2026-02-04 to 2026-05-04, for wallets with ≥ $500 volume in that window).

- **Positions:** those with `first_buy_ts` ≥ 2026-03-23 (the registered 6-week window). The study's day for each position = the day of its first buy.
- **Value:**
  - Resolved markets: held tokens are redeemed at their outcome prices.
  - Unresolved markets: held tokens are marked at the last hourly close before 2026-05-04 from `market_hour_bars.parquet`.
  - X = (sell_usd + held value) / buy_usd.
- **Known biases, stated in advance, both against H1/H2:**
  - Wallets under $500 volume are absent.
  - The fills-only ledger omits mint/merge/redeem legs. An earlier audit measured +2-4% of volume upward bias in P&L.
- **Bot / market-maker rule:** hourly fill counts are not in the table, so the rule becomes: exclude wallets with ≥ 1,000 fills and ≥ 80% maker fills (market makers), or with more than 5,000 fills in the window (bots). Positions where both outcomes are held in the same market (likely minted pairs) are reported as a sensitivity.

**Hyperliquid specifics:**
- Episodes are reconstructed per (wallet, coin) from `start` + signed `sz`.
- Funding comes from `hyperliquid-archive/asset_ctxs` hourly rates.
- The vault list comes from the public Hyperliquid info API (`vaultSummaries`).

**Listed-memecoin arm (secondary; design as registered in Study 1, "Listed memecoins").**
- **Population:** CoinGecko meme-category coins (active, 7,363) plus inactive coins with pump.fun-style Solana addresses (973). Dead coins are included through their contract addresses.
- **Bets:** K = 5 per coin.
  - Entry is uniform over [first pool's creation, 2026-10-02].
  - Hold is log-uniform over [1 min, 7 d].
- **Pricing:**
  - Price = close of the last traded minute at or before t, from the token's top pool (GeckoTerminal pool OHLCV via the CoinGecko API).
  - Coins with no on-chain address (e.g. DOGE) use CoinGecko aggregated prices: the hourly point at or before t.
  - Costs: 30 bp per side (sensitivity 10 / 100 bp).
- **Dead-pool sensitivity:** X = 0 when the exit price is more than 7 days stale.
- **Statistics:** Study 1's six statistics plus the four-condition ruling, so the listed arm is directly comparable with the launch tier.

## Amendment S3-A2 (2026-10-10, before any Study 3 outcome computed)

A complete on-chain record for the window was obtained, so all three venues now use the same calendar window and the same data layers.

**Polymarket primary:**
- **Window:** 2026-08-08 to 2026-09-18, the same as pump.fun and Hyperliquid.
- **Fills:** on-chain fills for every wallet, from an existing on-chain fill feed.
- **Side legs:** mint, merge, split and redeem events from the Conditional Tokens contract events for the same window, so cost basis and payouts are complete. This removes the fills-only bias noted in S3-A1.
- **Resolutions:** market metadata and resolutions from Polymarket's public market data.
- **Positions and rules:**
  - Position = (wallet, outcome token).
  - Cash in = USDC paid for buys + USDC used to mint (split).
  - Cash out = USDC from sells + USDC from merges and redemptions inside the window + the value of tokens held at window end (resolved: payout value; unresolved: last trade price before 2026-09-18 15:17 UTC).
- **Retail filter:** the registered hourly rule (> 200 fills in any clock hour) and the maker rule (≥ 80% maker, ≥ 1,000 fills), now computable from fill timestamps.

**Demoted:** the S3-A1 table (Feb-May 2026, ≥ $500 wallets, fills-only) becomes a replication footnote, as the July pump.fun slice is.

**Not used, to keep the arms even:** order books, Kalshi, and earlier years. They have no counterpart in the other venues.

## Amendment S3-A3 (2026-10-11, before any Polymarket outcome statistic was computed)

Validation of the Polymarket inputs (`code/s3_validate.py` and further checks) found two gaps.

1. **The fill feed has no negative-risk trades.** The feed contains no fills in negative-risk (multi-outcome) markets: 0 fills across 732,294 negative-risk tokens. API spot checks: 58 of 235 trades in 8 random wallets were missing, and every classifiable missing trade was negative-risk.
2. **The token map is incomplete.** The 2026-09-19 market snapshot maps only 20,697 of 827,745 traded tokens; weekly snapshots hold only markets updated that week.

Remedies:
- **Token map and resolutions:** built from every weekly snapshot 2026-07-02 to 2026-09-19 (later snapshots only if tokens remain unmapped). The latest record per market with `updated_at` at or before the window end is used for resolution state. A token in a market not closed by the window end is valued at its last trade price.
- **Negative-risk fills:** taken from Polymarket's public orderbook subgraph (Goldsky), only if a one-day test shows that it includes negative-risk exchange fills and reproduces the public trade API for sampled wallets.
- **Fallback, if the test fails:** the Polymarket arm is restricted to standard (non-negative-risk) markets, and this is stated as a scope limit.

## Amendment S3-A4 (2026-10-11, before any Polymarket outcome statistic was computed)

The Goldsky subgraph named in S3-A3 is deprecated, following Polymarket's migration to V2 exchange contracts. Polymarket fills are therefore backfilled from the Polygon chain itself via an Alchemy RPC endpoint.

**Contracts in the window** (blocks 91,628,115 to 94,047,311), identified from ERC-1155 transfer operators on the Conditional Tokens contract:
- V2 exchange `0xe111180000d2663c0091e4f400237545b87b996b`: the one the existing fill feed captures.
- V2 exchange `0xe2222d279d744050d28e00520010520000310f59`: identical bytecode, absent from that feed, presumed negative-risk.
- Negative-risk adapter `0xd91e80cf2e7be2e162c6513ced06f1dd0da35296`.
- The V1 exchanges are near idle.

**Method:**
1. Decode the V2 fill event against the feed's own decoded rows for the same transactions.
2. **Gate:** a one-day pull of `0xe111…` must reproduce the feed rows exactly (same tx, address, side, USD and token amounts).
3. Then pull `0xe222…` and the adapter for the full window.
4. Polymarket fills = feed (`0xe111…`) + chain-decoded `0xe222…`.

**Completeness check:** the share of Polymarket data-API trades (sampled wallets) absent from the combined fills must fall to under 2%. Otherwise the arm is restricted to the covered contracts, and that is stated.

## Amendment S3-A5 (2026-10-11, before any Polymarket outcome statistic was computed)

The Alchemy backfill registered in S3-A4 was completed as a validated dataset. The Polymarket arm now uses it. No Polymarket outcome statistic has been computed on any source.

**Fills source:** the completed on-chain fill dataset, 2026-08-08 to 2026-09-18 UTC. Timestamps are block times. It carries every V2 and legacy exchange plus `fee` and `exchange` columns. The S3-A4 gate and completeness bar were met on this dataset:
- `0xe111…` rows equal the earlier feed row for row (2026-09-01 01:00 UTC hour, 124,840/124,840);
- `0xe222…` rows equal our own decode of the same hour (36,771/36,771);
- 175,937 raw OrderFilled logs in 12 random windows show 0 missing and 0 extra;
- data-API trades missing from the fills: 0 of 204 in 21 sampled wallets, excluding combos (below).

**Fees** are now included:
- pUSD exchanges (`0xe111…`, `0xe222…`): a buy costs `usd_amount + fee`, and a sell returns `usd_amount − fee`.
- Legacy exchanges: the fee is paid in the asset received. A buy receives `token_amount − fee` tokens; a sell receives `usd_amount − fee` USDC.

**Combo exchange excluded.** `0xe3333700ca9d93003f00f0f71f8515005f6c00aa` (parlay/combo tokens, live since 2026-05-26) was found after S3-A4. A combo token pays only if every leg resolves its way. It is a different instrument from a single market and has no single-market resolution.
- Combo fills are excluded from the primary.
- Their share of window USD volume and of wallets is reported.
- Wallets that trade combos keep their single-market positions.

**Side legs:**
- **CTF events** as in S3-A2: user-level splits and merges, where the stakeholder also appears as a fill address.
- **Neg-risk user flows** from `negrisk_adapter_events` (NegRiskAdapter `0xd91e…` and CollateralAdapter `0xada2005600…`), under the same user rule.
  - NegRiskAdapter rows whose stakeholder is `0xada2005600…` duplicate the CollateralAdapter's own row and are dropped.
  - A split or merge of `amount` on a question counts as `amount` USDC, allocated `amount / 2` to each of the question's two outcome tokens. This is S3-A2's rule with n_out = 2.
- **Conversions** (`PositionsConverted`): the user gives `amount` NO tokens on each of k questions and receives `amount` YES tokens on each of the other questions plus (k − 1) × `amount` USDC, net of the conversion fee.
  - The USDC is cash out, split equally across the k NO positions, and those NO tokens leave the positions.
  - The YES tokens received enter with zero cash in.
  - Sensitivity: positions touched by a conversion are dropped.
- **Redemptions are not added separately.** A token held at window end in a market resolved by then is valued at its payout. That equals the redemption amount, so adding redemptions would double-count them.

**Resolutions and token map:** a cumulative market metadata table, current rows of the 2026-10-03 build. It is cumulative, and its truncated weekly snapshots are handled by carry-forward.
- A token is resolved if `resolved_at` ≤ 2026-09-18 15:17 UTC. Its payout is `resolution_price`. The resolution time is the market's actual close time, so using a later build adds no lookahead.
- Otherwise the token is valued at its last fill price at or before that time.
- Tokens absent from the master (unlisted markets, about 0.005% of volume) are valued the same way.

**Unchanged:** positions, retail filters, window and H1-H5 are as registered.

## Erratum S3-E1 (2026-10-11, after first results were computed and seen)

A blind code audit found the defects below in the first Study 3 run (commit 3300d2a). The first-run numbers are superseded. They are kept in the repo history and reported as such. Each fix restores the registered definition and does not change it.

1. **Calendar day (all venues).** `s3_analyze.py` computed the day index as `((t − w0) // 86400)::int`. In DuckDB, `//` on a DOUBLE is true division, and the integer cast rounds, so days ran noon to noon. Fixed to `floor((t − w0) / 86400)`, which gives UTC calendar days as registered. This affects the H1 day clusters and the H4/H5 strata.
2. **Halves (all venues).** The midpoint is now the midpoint of each venue's own window, from 2026-08-08 00:00 UTC to the venue's last event used. The first run used a fixed 2026-09-19 end.
3. **H5 null.** The null distribution now covers both registered statistics: the Spearman correlation and the top-decile half-2 RTP. Row order within a wallet is made deterministic.
4. **Polymarket, NULL payouts.**
   - 4,579 tokens are marked resolved with no resolution price, most likely void or 50/50 resolutions. They are now valued at last fill price, the registered fallback.
   - Positions with no tokens left at window end no longer get a NULL end value. In the first run, 58,363 such positions were dropped.
5. **Polymarket, conversions.** The YES tokens a conversion yields entered positions with zero cash in, and the analysis then dropped those positions (cash in must be > 0). Their value ($2.7M) was lost. Now the window-end value of the YES tokens is credited, together with the conversion's USDC, as cash out of the k converted NO positions, split equally.
   - Seven conversions whose index sets exceed 128 bits, and 17 whose market is absent from the market metadata table, are left out.
   - YES tokens of questions absent from the market metadata table are not credited (13.3M token units, mostly placeholders).
6. **Polymarket, binary-market collateral adapter.** `0xada100db00ca00073811820692005400218fce1f` routes user splits, merges and redemptions in standard markets. These appear in ctf_events with the adapter, not the user, as stakeholder. In the window: splits $279M, merges $423M. The adapter's own events, which name the user, are being added. Once they are available, those user splits and merges enter as side legs under the S3-A5 rule (amount / 2 per token).
   - Until then the Polymarket arm is reported as provisional.

**Checked by the audit and refuted:** 17 v2 block ranges with no files inside the window. They are confirmed zero-fill windows of Polymarket's order book: raw `eth_getLogs` over each full range returns no Polymarket OrderFilled.

**S3-E1 item 5, corrected before any computation under S3-E1.** Crediting window-end YES value to the NO positions breaks token accounting when a user later trades the converted YES tokens. Conversions are therefore booked as an exchange at market:
- each YES token received enters its position with cash in = `amount` × its last fill price at or before the conversion (0 if it never traded);
- the k converted NO positions receive, split equally, cash out = (k − 1) × `amount` USDC + the total market value of those YES tokens.

Tokens and value are both conserved.

## Erratum S3-E2 (2026-10-12, after S3-E1 results were computed and seen)

A second blind code audit covered `s2_positions.py`, `hl_episodes.py`, the listed-arm code and `s3_secondary.py`. The defects below are corrected. Each fix restores the registered definition. The S3-E1 numbers for the affected arms are superseded and stay in the repo history.

1. **Memecoin positions (also Study 2).** Duplicate removal on `payload_hash` ran inside each trade file, but two pairs of upstream batch files overlap (2026-08-08 21:15 to 08-10, and 08-31 19:57 onward). About 4.7% of curve trade rows and 1.4% of PumpSwap swaps were counted twice. This produced phantom residual tokens, wrong `oversold` flags and doubled hourly activity counts.
   - **Fix:** trade files and swap files with overlapping time ranges are merged into groups and deduplicated across each group. Curve trades are keyed on `payload_hash`; swaps on (slot, tx index, trader, mints, amounts). Activity counts are taken from the deduplicated rows.
   - The 50-fills/hour sensitivity is computed on hourly counts after merging all parts.
2. **Hyperliquid liquidation flag.** It marked every fill that carried a liquidation record, which includes the counterparty and auto-deleveraged positions.
   - **Fix:** an episode counts as liquidated only if it contains a crossed (taker-side) liquidation fill that is not an `Auto-Deleveraging` fill.
3. **Hyperliquid scope.** Coins with a `#` prefix (outcome contracts) are not core perps and are now excluded, as `:`, `@` and `/` coins already were.
4. **Listed-memecoin arm.**
   - Prices are re-fetched so the candle opens at or before t; the old request returned candles up to 60 s after t.
   - The token side is set explicitly, so a coin quoted as the pool's quote token is priced correctly.
   - Failed price requests are retried and logged. Unpriced bets are reported by cause, not dropped silently.
5. **Documented, not changed:**
   - Hyperliquid funding is missing for 95 of 1,008 window hours (funding there is zero; total funding is 0.38 bp of stake).
   - Memecoin tokens moved to another wallet are still valued as held. A sensitivity using the transfer data available (2026-08-14 to 09-08) is reported.
